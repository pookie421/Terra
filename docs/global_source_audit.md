# Global source and API audit

Updated 13 November 2026 against the implementation and linked primary documentation. Source metadata and a bounded single-coordinate NASA/NISAR check support the new integration; they are not a global availability sweep or predictive validation. TERRA-SHIELD targets global operation competitive with LHASA 2.0. Input credibility and sustained coverage are part of that target.

## Are the numbers real?

There are several different answers. A provider payload can be authentic while its value is a model prediction rather than a local measurement. A derived quantity can be computed correctly while being physically inappropriate at its displayed scale. A reference prior can be useful without being observed or independently validated.

The current system mixes compiled terrain, numerical weather, soil predictions, event catalogs, image-color proxies and project assumptions. It does **not** currently have direct global measurements of cohesion, friction, regolith depth or groundwater head. Those variables must remain explicit conditional estimates, with uncertainty and provenance. Their absence does not justify disabling the physical column or routing. Conversely, computing a complete column does not establish that its parameters are locally correct.

The largest current weaknesses are assumed strength/depth/hydrologic coefficients, coarse forcing applied to finer cells, uncertain initiation labels, weather-selected absence controls, and retrospective inputs that may follow failure. More APIs alone cannot resolve these weaknesses. Provider-channel counts do not measure the number of independent observing systems.

## Source-by-source findings

| Input and implementation | Evidence type and critical finding | Required improvement or acceptance evidence |
|---|---|---|
| AWS Terrarium; `dem_processor.py` | Compiled elevation tiles, followed by project derivatives. Metric axes and aspect are corrected. Tile pixel spacing/encoding precision do not establish terrain accuracy. TWI uses a fixed 1 km contributing-path support, not an entire watershed. The elevation >0.5 m land mask can discard real low land; Web Mercator has polar limits. | Record source mosaic/vintage where available, vertical datum, resolution and voids; compare derivatives/drainage with an established GIS on independent terrain; use a proper land mask; test coasts, antimeridian and latitude limits. [Provider registry](https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/) and [upstream attribution](https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md). |
| Open-Meteo forcing; `global_ingestor.py`, `rainfall_features.py`, `forecast_weather.py` | Numerical model products, rather than a rain gauge at every requested coordinate. Latest-24h and preceding-144h liquid windows now reject gaps; forecast rain+showers and archive rain have distinct definitions. A common extractor does not remove model, resolution or assimilation differences. One scan-center weather series is shared over the terrain scan. | Persist the actual upstream model/run and native support, issue/valid times and revision; validate rainfall extremes against independent gauges/radar where available; benchmark archived forecasts as issued. Reanalysis and later event-day weather must remain separately labeled retrospective experiments. [Forecast definitions](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs), [archive](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/historical-weather-api) and [historical forecasts](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/historical-forecast-api). |
| NASA POWER `GWETROOT`/`GWETPROF`; `global_ingestor.py` | Coarse modeled relative wetness context. POWER's meteorological grid is about 0.5° × 0.625°; its numbers are not a local volumetric sample, observed saturation or piezometric head. NASA branding does not resolve the support mismatch. This feed is not SMAP merely because both concern moisture. | Keep index units and product identity explicit, retain the observation/product date independently of retrieval time, avoid duplicate evidence from shared model ancestry, and compare candidate moisture products on the same latency/coverage protocol. [POWER source/accuracy FAQ](https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/faqs/data/), [meteorological grid and hydrology sources](https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/methodology/data/sources_hydrology/) and [MERRA-2 moisture definitions](https://gs6101-gmao.gsfc.nasa.gov/gmao-products/merra-2/faq_merra-2/). |
| SoilGrids WCS; `soilgrids.py` | Globally predicted properties, nominally 250 m, with mapped uncertainty. Current ingestion averages mean predictions across a 3 km patch and thickness-weights 0–30 cm; it does not retrieve per-cell borehole strength or deeper regolith. Bulk density conversion is ×10 to kg/m³, SOC ÷10 to g/kg, texture g/kg ÷1000 to fractions. Sharing the patch across a scan removes local variation. WCS success is not validation of the pedotransfer output. | Preserve depth/support and property-level provenance. Evaluate deeper layers for the modeled column, retrieve quantiles, propagate uncertainty/correlation and compare local observations. ISRIC itself advises macro-regional/global use rather than local/farm inference. [Layer units/depths/quantiles](https://docs.isric.org/globaldata/soilgrids/SoilGrids_faqs_01.html), [access policy](https://docs.isric.org/globaldata/soilgrids/SoilGrids_faqs_02.html), [scale guidance](https://docs.isric.org/globaldata/soilgrids/SoilGrids_faqs_04.html). |
| Topographic soil fallback; `soilgrids.py` | Project slope/curvature/TWI prior. It is a reproducible assumption, not a second soil observation. Corrected per-cell priors prevent viewport normalization, but dependence on terrain can be counted again in mechanics and ML. | Keep fallback provenance per field; test prior sensitivity, correlation and out-of-domain behavior. Never record this fallback as a successful SoilGrids measurement. |
| Strength, depth, conductivity, diffusivity and pressure; `reference_context.py`, `multi_causal_engine.py` | Hydraulic/mechanical equation families have citations; texture-to-strength, slope-to-depth, basal drainage, ET, initialization and covariance values include **project assumptions**. Surface moisture has shallow support; it cannot directly observe a deep slip-plane head. FOSM propagates declared parameter spread; it does not discover omitted structural uncertainty. | Maintain a coefficient register with units, domain and derivation. Compare priors with independent soil/depth/groundwater datasets; test hydrologic trajectories and sensitivity coverage. Replace assumptions when evidence supports a better global prior, retaining a usable reference column and disclosed intervals. [Active methods](physics_sources.md). |
| USGS FDSN; `global_ingestor.py` | Catalog events are reported source parameters. The local PGA is a project magnitude/distance attenuation proxy, not station motion or ShakeMap. The 300-second catalog window now avoids treating an old earthquake as simultaneous load, but catalog publication latency means this is not a real-time shaking detector. Thirty-day history represents background context. | Use event revisions and origin/report times, source rupture/site effects and appropriate ground-motion uncertainty; evaluate ShakeMap or validated GMPE inputs where available. Do not treat no recently reported earthquake as proof of no shaking. [FDSN specification](https://earthquake.usgs.gov/fdsnws/event/1/), [USGS ShakeMap uncertainty](https://www.usgs.gov/publications/quantifying-and-qualifying-usgs-shakemap-uncertainty). |
| COOLR and retained inventories; `coolr.py`, dataset builders | Authentic reports and mapped inventories have different timing, location, completeness and geometry. A report pin or footprint centroid may be far from initiation. Lack of a report is not observed slope stability. Preserving 71 unresolved extents avoids invented crowns, but the remaining point proxies are still imperfect. | Audit original inventory methods/licenses, timing and positional uncertainty; deduplicate events; separate initiation/runout/exposure targets; acquire representative wet non-events and quantify reporting bias. [NASA description](https://gpm.nasa.gov/applications/landslides/coolr). |
| EONET fire/landslide context; `natural_events.py` | Event metadata for general information and visualization. NASA explicitly cautions that spatial/temporal extents are approximate. A fire location/proximity is not local burn severity, a complete perimeter, or a complete event inventory. | Preserve geometry/date uncertainty; use dedicated severity/perimeter products with acquisition times before physical post-fire claims. No event returned must remain distinct from verified absence. [EONET disclaimer](https://eonet.gsfc.nasa.gov/what-is-eonet). |
| Esri World Imagery RGB; `satellite_processor.py` | Real image colors, with a visible-band vegetation transform and bare/char/proximity heuristic. They are not NIR NDVI, dNBR, root cohesion or calibrated burn severity. Mosaic acquisition dates can differ from retrieval date and event date. Current greenness/proximity coefficients are project choices. | Retrieve acquisition/provider/resolution metadata; use timed multispectral reflectance and dedicated fire products for those physical claims; validate proxy usefulness through ablations. [Esri imagery metadata guidance](https://support.esri.com/en-us/knowledge-base/how-to-view-the-world-imagery-basemap-metadata-in-arcgi-000018129). |
| Macrostrat; `ground_context.py` | Mapped bedrock description. The first returned map unit need not represent local regolith, discontinuity geometry, weathering or measured shear strength. Map scales and source coverage vary. | Retain original map scale/source/license, resolve overlaps deliberately and validate any lithology-to-prior transformation independently. [API](https://macrostrat.org/docs/data-services), [map sources and scales](https://www.macrostrat.org/docs/maps). |
| GloFAS via Open-Meteo; `ground_context.py` | Modeled river discharge at roughly 5 km support; `cell_selection=land` does not identify a surveyed channel/toe intersection. The current bounded surge ratio uses an available past-week minimum with a denominator floor; it is a project index, not a recurrence interval, bank stress or erosion rate. | Match a routed river reach and slope toe; retain model/version and ensemble information; validate against gauges and channel/bank geometry before converting flow to destabilization. [Flood API](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/flood-api). |
| OSM/Overpass; `osm_geometry.py`, `global_ingestor.py` | Contributed mapped assets with incomplete and geographically unequal coverage. Geometry intersections and unique IDs improve counting; they do not establish occupancy, vulnerability, road-cut geometry or actual loss. Mirror failover improves access, not data completeness. | Record data/snapshot age, geometry validity and coverage; compare independent asset sources where possible. Zero mapped assets is not zero exposed population. Plan supported bulk/replication ingestion for global use. [Overpass service guidance](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Overpass_API), [OSM attribution and terms](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). |
| Open-Meteo ensembles; `forecast_weather.py` | Weather-member distributions represent a model ensemble. They do not by themselves calibrate landslide probability or bound material/structural uncertainty. | Audit member selection, dependence, issue time, alignment and forecast reliability; evaluate the complete propagated system. [Ensemble API](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/ensemble-api). |
| NASA LHASA/Global Landslide Susceptibility ImageServers; `nasa_lhasa.py` | Authentic modeled raster output at native 30-arcsecond support, sampled once per source pixel and reused over H3 cells. Susceptibility is categorical 0–5, not probability. Hazard coverage is approximately 60°S–60°N; the susceptibility product has a different extent. The public service metadata leaves exact served model version and issue/valid time undeclared. | Preserve native pixels, valid zeros, NoData/domain masks, timing unknowns and product identity. Do not attach the current upstream repository version to an unversioned service, infer 30 m predictions from repeated pixels, or treat Tomorrow as an exact T+24h forecast. The outage fallback is an unvalidated project index with its own coefficients and missing inputs, never official NASA probability. [NASA implementation](https://github.com/nasa/LHASA), [native multipoint sampling](https://developers.arcgis.com/rest/services-reference/enterprise/get-samples/). |
| NISAR CMR/ASF discovery and optional L-SAR subsets; `nisar_processor.py`, `radar_context.py` | Public granule metadata establishes discovery, footprint, acquisition/product maturity and links. It does not observe deformation. Optional masked/georeferenced GeoTIFF, HDF5 or bounded authenticated COG subsets can supply coherence, documented LOS time-series velocity and an uncalibrated HV/HH power ratio. A repeat-pair phase is not an annual velocity; the ratio is not volumetric soil moisture. | Retain collection/granule IDs, acquisition interval, radar band, units, geometry, references and quality. Use actual footprints and respect provisional/beta versions. Require a documented granule-anchored site calibration before converting radar evidence into cohesion; metadata alone retains the full existing physical prior. The active classifier is not SAR-trained. Never add current NISAR acquisitions to historical event-time training rows. [ASF product/discovery guide](https://nisar-docs.asf.alaska.edu/earthaccess/) and [data products](https://nisar-docs.asf.alaska.edu/data-products/). |

## LHASA 2.0 as an actual comparator

The [2021 LHASA 2.0 study](https://doi.org/10.3389/feart.2021.640043) uses XGBoost with rainfall, antecedent wetness/snow and static terrain/lithology. It constructs daily 30-arcsecond targets, uses IMERG and SMAP L4, and evaluates later inventories separately from development data. It also documents latency, missing-data, inventory and domain limits, including a 60°S–60°N evaluation domain. LHASA 2.0 is not a direct borehole-based physical column, and should not be cited as evidence that it performs Terra's FOSM/runout workflow.

Terra currently has different inputs, labels, control sampling, resolution and evaluation design. Its 186-row balanced CV cannot establish superiority over that study. Pin [NASA's code/product release](https://github.com/nasa/LHASA), reproduce its declared task, evaluate both systems on common data/support and report full coverage separately. Candidate IMERG/SMAP integration needs its own source audit; those products are not present merely because other NASA endpoints respond.

The selectable live NASA comparison now exposes real ImageServer values and their support alongside Terra's estimates. Its fallback uses exposed project rainfall scales and mean terrain over the scan-covered coarse bins; it does not reproduce the published model, IMERG/SMAP inputs or learned weights. Mixed official and fallback values remain labeled separately, including separate peaks and official-first critical-cell selection. Neither the switcher nor the fallback establishes LHASA parity. H3 resolution 9 averages about 0.105 km² per aggregation cell; the nominal DEM pixel size does not make those cells 30 m predictions. [H3 resolution statistics](https://h3geo.org/docs/core-library/restable/).

Radar context is versioned separately as `nisar_context_features_v1`; the approved 25-feature artifact and benchmark cohorts are unchanged. Missing radar leaves strain unconstrained and keeps broad material/state FOSM priors. Widening a factor-of-safety envelope by an arbitrary multiplier would invent quantified information just as surely as treating absent radar as a measured zero. A valid site-calibrated cohesion value follows the existing sourced-ground-input path; its changed physical features can affect the current model, with that provenance exposed. Independent calibration, uncertainty coverage and a genuinely trained SAR feature model remain work to validate.

The [global benchmark protocol](../ROADMAP.md#2-freeze-a-fair-global-benchmark) requires attainable false-alarm budgets, representative wet backgrounds, issue-time data, separated events/regions/time and uncertainty. Multi-hazard/runout additions need separate evaluation. This is a global competition target; demonstrations at a handful of locations cannot substitute for it.

## Required record for future source or coefficient changes

Every new or changed field should state:

1. **Origin:** product, upstream institution, version, license/access terms, source URL and a retained payload hash or reproducible identifier.
2. **Meaning:** observed/retrieved/modeled/mapped/derived/prior/scenario; units, native spatial support, depth and temporal aggregation.
3. **Timing:** issue time, valid/observation time, retrieval time, normal latency, revision policy and stale-cache behavior. Future information must not enter an earlier prediction.
4. **Transformation:** interpolation, aggregation, unit conversion, clipping, assumed coefficients and shared upstream dependencies. Requested H3 resolution is recorded separately from native evidence resolution.
5. **Failure:** null/sentinel/zero semantics, partial coverage, provider errors, fallback provenance and effects on uncertainty. Preserve valid zero measurements without turning missing data into dry/stable conditions.
6. **Validation:** contract fixtures check software behavior; independent measurements, external solver comparisons and held-out skill check scientific adequacy. Supply both where applicable, retaining failed cases and exclusions.
7. **Global delivery:** supported domain, measured completeness/freshness, rate limits, storage/refresh costs and an acquisition strategy compatible with repeated global operation.

This contract is partly implemented, with the gaps above still open. Do not describe the whole checklist as shipped. Prior-based physical computation remains useful while the evidence improves; uncertainty intervals must remain labeled as conditional on their assumptions.
